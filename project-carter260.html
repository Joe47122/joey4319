01 · Concept Brief

Synthesising Two Mid-Size Platforms

The Carter Fusion 260 combines the cabin volume and long-range cruise capability of the Bombardier Challenger 650 with the short-field performance and aerodynamic efficiency of the Gulfstream G280.

Inspired by the Boeing 787 structural philosophy, the airframe specifies CFRP composite primary structure in place of conventional aluminium alloys to improve strength-to-weight ratio, fatigue resistance, and cabin pressure differential.
