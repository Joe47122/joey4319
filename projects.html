Asymmetric Aerodynamic Structural Analysis of ATR 72-600 Using the Actuator Method

Modelled the complete ATR 72-600 (wing, fuselage, nacelles, empennage) in CATIA V5 and simulated it in ANSYS Fluent with ADMv2 and k-ω SST across take-off (116 KCAS, 10° AoA) and OEI climb (115 KCAS, 6° AoA) on a 616,523-element poly-hexcore mesh, verified through a three-level Grid Convergence Index study. Resolved a −6,752 Pa suction peak and 35.9 m²/s² TKE at the operative wing-nacelle junction in ~3 CPU hours.
